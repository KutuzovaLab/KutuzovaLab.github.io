The group

We are a research group established in September 2026, led by Assistant Professor Svetlana Kutuzova.

We are a CS lab developing deep learning methods in the domains of generative modeling, probabilistic modeling and multimodal deep learning.

We tackle important life science problems and highly value our experimental and bioinformatics collaborators. Our favorite data currently are: untargeted metabolomics, mass specs and chromatograms, metagenome sequencing, Electronic Healthcare Records. We are always happy to collaborate on new problems and datatypes.

The current grant of €1.6 million from the Novo Nordisk Foundation allows us to dig deep into closing the methodological gaps with detecting and identifying harmful chemicals in water. More specifically, we develop novel generative models on untargeted chromatography–mass spectrometry data. We closely collaborate with the Jan H. Christensen analytical chemistry lab and their vast collection of Danish water samples.
